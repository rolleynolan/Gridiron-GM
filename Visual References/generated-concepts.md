- Shared v2 prompt method: primary helmet decal for a fictional professional American-football franchise; professional sports silhouette first, then polished small-grid 32-bit translation; one dominant idea, broad shapes, minimal interior detail, transparent background, flat team colors, 32-pixel readability. Excluded wordmarks, scene art, app/clip-art treatment, enclosing badges, footballs, real-team resemblance, glow, gradients, blur, antialiasing, bevels, shadows, 3D, and photorealism.
