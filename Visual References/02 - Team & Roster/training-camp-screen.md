# Training Camp Screen

## Role

Training Camp is a dense roster-management workspace for setting development emphasis and practice opportunity during preseason. It does not duplicate the depth chart, turn practice into a daily drill minigame, or manufacture a formal position-battle score.

## Player focus

The default Player Focus view shows the full camp roster with player, position, age, experience, overall, scouting confidence, health, selected training focus, and first-team practice-rep share. Ordinary team screens label the summary rating simply **OVR**; estimate qualifiers are reserved for actual scouting-report contexts. Training focus is edited directly in the table. First-team reps use compact numerical controls and remain bounded within the applicable position group. Readiness is not a separate table column.

## Position groups and reports

Position Groups provides the corresponding group-level training emphasis and rep allocation. Weekly Reports holds dated staff observations, emerging position-battle assessments, preseason evidence, and any delegated depth changes. Competitions emerge from roster composition, readiness, performance, and actual opportunity; the GM does not manually create battles or receive a synthetic winner score.

## Control

The screen always shows whether the GM or coaching staff controls training decisions. Delegation is reversible and uses the same underlying focus and rep model. Health restrictions remain authoritative, changes save immediately, and neither a focus nor a rep allocation guarantees development or a depth-chart outcome.
