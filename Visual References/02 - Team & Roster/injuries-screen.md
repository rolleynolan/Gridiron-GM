# Injuries Screen Concept

## Layout

The Injuries screen is a simple three-panel workspace. Three tall vertical tiles share the available screen width and each contains a scrollable roster list.

1. **Healthy / Available** — all healthy players, showing their current health or availability status.
2. **Injured** — players currently injured but not on injured reserve, showing the injury and expected recovery length.
3. **Injured Reserve** — players on IR, showing the injury and expected recovery length.

## Row content and interaction

- Every row identifies the player, position, current status, and the information relevant to its panel.
- Injured and IR rows make the injury description and recovery timeline immediately scannable.
- Selecting a player opens the player profile for more detailed health history and context.
- The three-way separation is the primary organizational tool; the page should avoid unnecessary charts, tiles, or extra dashboard complexity.

## Visual direction

Use clear, restrained availability status cues alongside text. Injury severity and timeline should be easy to scan without relying solely on color. The screen retains the same compact FM/OOTP list treatment as Roster, but its panel structure makes roster availability understandable in one glance.
