# Team Info Screen Concept

## Role

Team Info is the current franchise reference screen. It complements Team History by showing the team's present identity,
ownership, stadium, facility grades, active owner projects, current organizational context, and useful routes to related
financial and historical records.

## Content

- Current team identity, conference/division, city, stadium, capacity, ownership, and other persisted franchise facts.
- Compact facility grades for the approved player-facility categories, plus stadium and Operations summaries once those
  catalogs are finalized. Grades are descriptive or clearly scaled and always explain the applicable functional context.
- Active or recently completed owner projects with funding status, progress, estimated completion, and disruption.
- Owner priorities, current expectations, and approved or pending facility discussions where appropriate.
- Links to Team Finances and Accounting for facility operating costs, owner funding, and project spending. Team Info does not
  duplicate those financial tables.

## Authority and interaction

Team Info is informational. The GM may route to the existing owner conversation/meeting workflow to express a preference or
review a decision, but cannot directly buy, approve, fund, maintain, or start a project from this screen. Announced,
requested, funded/in-progress, completed, and uncommitted states remain distinct.

## Visual direction

Use a compact encyclopedia-style franchise overview with a small amount of team/stadium identity and dense factual tables.
Facility grades and project states should be easy to scan without turning the page into a construction dashboard. Costs live
in Finances; history lives in Team History.
