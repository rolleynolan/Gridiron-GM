# Team Standings Screen Concept

## Role

Team Standings is the franchise-focused standings view. It answers *where are we in the division and playoff race?* without making the player scan the entire league.

## Scope

- Show the user’s division standings prominently, with the user’s team clearly identified.
- Show the relevant conference playoff picture and seeding context.
- Surface the team’s current position, record, games behind or equivalent gap, and postseason status where applicable.
- Provide direct routes to the broader League Standings screen when the player wants the complete league view.

League Standings, under the **League** navigation group, remains the authoritative full-league table for every division and conference. Team Standings is the focused, franchise-relevant shortcut.

## Visual direction

Use a dense, readable standings-table treatment with modest emphasis on the user’s team and key playoff cut lines. The design should make the competitive context immediately legible, not turn standings into a decorative dashboard.
