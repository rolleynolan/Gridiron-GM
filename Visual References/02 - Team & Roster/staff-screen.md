# Staff Screen Concept

## Role

Staff is an OOTP-style organizational overview. It answers *who currently fills each staff role, and what kind of staff member are they?* The page is organized around positions in the franchise rather than around a generic sortable personnel database.

## Layout

- A compact `Team > Staff` header identifies the franchise staff organization and holds any hiring/management actions.
- The main workspace is a single tall, scrollable list of staff positions, ordered into logical departments where helpful.
- Each row represents a **staff role** and displays the person currently assigned to it.
- Empty or temporarily unfilled roles remain visible in the list so organizational gaps are clear.

## Row content

Every staff-position row shows:

| Staff Position | Assigned Staff Member | Tendency | Aptitude in Current Role |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

- **Staff Position:** for example, head coach, coordinator, position coach, scout, or another supported franchise role.
- **Assigned Staff Member:** name, with a route to the individual staff profile.
- **Tendency:** the relevant coaching or scouting tendency for that role.
- **Aptitude in Current Role:** a clear assessment of how well the staff member suits their current assignment.

Selecting a row or staff member opens a more detailed profile with their history, contract, attributes, and effects. For the head coach and coordinators, the profile is also the home for that coach’s unique strategy/gameplan and style; strategy is not a separate Team screen. The main Staff screen itself stays focused on the current staff structure and the information needed to evaluate it quickly.

## Visual direction

Use compact list rows, department dividers, and a clear role-to-person relationship—the familiar OOTP staffing-directory feel. This is an organization chart expressed as a scrollable, information-dense list, not a general-purpose staff spreadsheet.

## Vacancies, promotions, and outside interview requests

Vacancies remain visible as ordinary role rows in this organization view, including the function currently reduced or suspended and the person temporarily covering the responsibility. Hiring routes to the Staff Market. An eligible internal staff member can be offered a promotion from their contextual staff action; the new role and any contract change are reviewed together, and the prior assignment becomes vacant only when the promotion resolves.

Requests from other franchises to interview a contracted assistant for a promotion arrive as Action Required Inbox messages rather than creating another Staff dashboard. The request identifies the current and requested roles, contract, permitted interview window, team-season status, and the coach's stated interest. Granting permission allows only the interview; denying it retains the coach but may have an explainable relationship consequence. Neither choice secretly hires, releases, or moves the staff member.
