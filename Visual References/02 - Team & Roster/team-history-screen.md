# Team History Screen Concept

## Role

Team History is the franchise encyclopedia: a compact reference page that explains the team's identity, achievements,
records, important people, and progression across eras. It should make a long-running save feel like an accumulated football
history rather than presenting only a season spreadsheet.

## Encyclopedia overview

The default Overview combines several compact reference sections:

- **Franchise identity:** founding year, home city, conference/division, stadium, current owner, team colors, nicknames, and
  other persisted identity facts.
- **Historical summary:** all-time record, playoff appearances, division and conference titles, championships, and concise
  era context drawn only from persisted history.
- **Honors:** league championships and team jersey retirements. There is no Ring of Honor.
- **Franchise records:** selected career, single-season, and single-game leaders with direct routes to the complete record book.
- **Notable figures:** championship contributors, long-tenured or record-setting players, and significant coaches and GMs.
- **Recent seasons:** a smaller chronological table showing recent records and postseason results, with access to the complete
  season archive and each season recap.

The overview resembles a well-organized sports encyclopedia entry: section headings, concise factual context, compact tables,
small portraits or trophy/jersey accents, and clear cross-links. It is not a marketing landing page or a wall of giant cards.
Do not add a Notable Eras section. Use that space for a longer recent-season table and more factual season columns.

## History navigation

Focused tabs or section links lead to **Overview**, **Seasons**, **Records**, **Honors & Retired Jerseys**, **Draft History**,
**Transactions**, **Staff History**, and **Financial History**. Deep archive screens retain dense sortable tables. Every
historical statement, leader, honor, and event is generated from persisted authoritative records rather than decorative lore.

## Visual direction

Use the accepted compact Gridiron GM shell and information density, but give this page more editorial hierarchy than Roster or
Depth Chart. Team branding, small historical images, trophy marks, and retired-number treatments may add identity without
overpowering the factual reference content. Championship and record milestones receive restrained gold distinction.
