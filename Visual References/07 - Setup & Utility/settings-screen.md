# Settings Screen Concept

## Navigation

In-career Settings uses four broad subheaders: **Global Options**, **Career Settings**, **Advance & Alerts**, and **Accessibility & Controls**. Global options apply across the application; career settings and advancement preferences persist only in the active career.

The accepted Career Settings difficulty layout is the reusable visual template for every settings view. Other settings screens swap the title, rows, descriptions, and appropriate controls inside the same full-width worksheet shell; they do not require separate bespoke mockups.

## Career difficulty

Difficulty is presented as a transparent full-width worksheet. Relaxed, Standard, Challenging, and Custom presets provide starting configurations, while each challenge area remains individually visible. The table identifies the current setting, its practical effect, when a change applies, and what it cannot change.

Initial visible areas are AI front-office decisions, negotiation, scouting uncertainty, owner pressure, and financial pressure. Difficulty never rigs results, changes the authoritative football simulation for the user, grants hidden opposing information, reveals true ratings, or scripts acceptance. Career-history records preserve difficulty changes. Save, discard, and restore-standard actions make pending changes explicit.

Ironwood Thunder and Career 1 are reference data only; production identity and settings belong to the active career.
