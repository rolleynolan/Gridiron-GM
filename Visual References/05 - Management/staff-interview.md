# Staff Candidate Interview

## Role

Every external staff hire begins with an interview before contract negotiation. The conversation explores how the candidate would work—scheme, philosophy, collaboration, requested resources, and job conditions—rather than unlocking ratings or traits. Staff qualifications and ratings are fully visible before the interview.

## Layout and interaction

The active interview uses one grounded 32-bit conference-room scene with the candidate, GM, and an applicable staff participant such as the Head Coach. A compact candidate strip shows role, age, experience, preferred scheme, philosophy, visible ratings, and expected contract. The current candidate response spans the page below the room.

Remaining role-specific questions appear directly beneath the response in a balanced two-column grid. Clicking a question immediately asks it; there is no Ask, Submit, or confirmation button, no question-category navigation, no applicant queue, and no persistent side panel. The GM may ask up to five questions or conclude early. The progress line shows only the number of questions used; an annual-limit callout is unnecessary.

## Outcome

Concluding produces a reviewable summary of working fit, stated requests, and contract demands. It does not reserve or hire the candidate and does not issue an offer. The Head Coach may participate and advise but cannot appoint staff. Any later offer and appointment require explicit GM review and normal availability validation.
