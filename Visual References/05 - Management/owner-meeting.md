# Annual Owner Meeting

## Role

The Annual Owner Meeting is a Football Manager-style board conversation covering season review, staff, operating budget, facilities, and next-season expectations. Staff review occurs inside this meeting rather than in a redundant post-meeting screen.

## Conversation structure

The meeting advances linearly through its agenda. Only the current topic appears, accompanied by a quiet topic number and the previously resolved topic; there are no agenda tabs, dropdowns, or controls for jumping ahead. Each topic must be resolved through dialogue before the meeting advances automatically. The active topic combines a restrained 32-bit conference-room scene, the current speaker's natural dialogue, directly clickable GM responses, and only the evidence needed for that discussion. There is no separate Ask or Submit button and no persistent question list.

## Staff review

Staff Review shows supporting context by department: role, staff member, actual season assessment, philosophy fit where applicable, contract state, and remaining dismissal cost. The owner and GM discuss retain, dismiss, extension, and budget direction. Discussion does not itself fire, extend, or hire anyone; resulting personnel actions remain separately reviewable and require explicit GM approval.

The meeting does not expose approval probabilities, owner thresholds, relationship meters, recommended answers, right/wrong feedback, or hidden ratings. Dismissal costs are franchise operating expenses rather than player-cap charges.
